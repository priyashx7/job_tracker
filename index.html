Visible table columns
Searching roles

The roles you are looking for at a company. These appear when you add or edit a company.

Person roles

The roles of the people you reach out to (recruiter, hiring manager, alumni and so on). These appear when you add or edit a person.

Person stages

Where you stand with each person, in order. These are independent of the company's overall status and sub-category, so you can pick any of them for any person.

Overall statuses and sub-categories

These describe the company as a whole. Each overall status has its own sub-categories (for example, a referral step after Applied). They do not limit which stage you can pick for a person.

Approach options

Channels available in the approach dropdown.
